Working alone, the end of the day does not announce itself. Nobody packs
up at a desk near mine. No commute resets the brain. If I do not close the
day deliberately, it leaks into the evening, the night, and the next
morning.

## The closing check

I do three things, in order, before I close the laptop:

- Read what I committed today. Not for review. To see the day from outside
  myself for a minute.
- Write one sentence about what tomorrow's first task will be. One
  sentence, on paper. Specific.
- Close every tab that is not relevant to that first task.

The whole thing takes four minutes.

## What it does

The next morning starts with a runway, not a search. I open the laptop and
the first task is already named. I do not lose the first hour to
re-establishing context.

## What goes wrong without it

I have skipped this check often enough to know the cost. The next day
starts with a vague sense of falling behind. The vague sense is wrong, but
it sets the tone for hours.
