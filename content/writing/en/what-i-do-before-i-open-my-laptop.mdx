The laptop is the last thing I open in the morning, not the first. This is
the only routine I have that I have not had to negotiate with myself about
for months.

## The order

I drink water before coffee. I write one sentence in a notebook — not a
journal entry, just one sentence about the thing I want to be true about
today. I walk for twenty minutes, with the phone on do-not-disturb. Then
I make coffee.

The laptop opens after the coffee is poured. That is roughly one hour from
waking.

## Why it holds

If I open the laptop first, my day becomes a response to what is in it.
Email, the dashboard, an overnight bug. None of that is unimportant, but
none of it is mine either.

The hour before the laptop is small but it is the only hour I get to write
on a blank page.

## What I changed

Nothing dramatic. I moved the laptop to a different table at night so it
is not the first thing I see. That single decision did most of the work.
