On a Tuesday in January I sat down at my desk, opened the mail app on
reflex, and then closed it without reading anything. I had not planned
to. I have not gone back to the old order since.

## The swap

For years my morning was: coffee, mail, then work. The mail step was an
average of forty minutes. It was forty minutes of responding to other
people's mornings before I had a chance to have my own.

The new order is: coffee, work, then mail. Mail at 11am, after the first
deep block. The first thing I do is the thing I decided last night, not
the thing someone emailed me overnight.

## What happens to the mail

It waits. The world has not, in five months, ended because I responded at
11am instead of 8am. Two real urgent things came up. Both were also on
Slack. Both were also a phone call.

The fear of being slow on mail was the fear that my mornings were owed to
the inbox. They were not.

## What I gained

The first deep block of the day, which used to be sometimes 11am,
sometimes 2pm, depending on the inbox. Now it is always 8am. That
consistency is worth more than the forty minutes itself.
