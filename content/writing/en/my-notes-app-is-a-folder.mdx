For a year I bounced between Notion, Obsidian, Bear, Apple Notes, and a
half-built thing I wrote myself. I came back to a folder of plain Markdown
files on iCloud Drive.

## What I gained

Search is grep. Backup is git. Sync is whatever filesystem the OS gives me.
When a tool I used to like changed its pricing or its sync model or its
default sort order, I noticed it as a small ripple. Now nothing ripples.

## What I lost

Some affordances. No backlinks unless I write them. No daily-note template
unless I touch-create the file. No graph view. I have not missed any of it.

## The honest version

The folder is not minimalism. It is debt avoidance. Every note tool I tried
had a future version of itself that I would have had to migrate from. A
folder of `.md` files has no future version.
